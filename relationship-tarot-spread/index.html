How to read this relationship spread

The three positions keep the reflection focused: You explores your own experience; The other person represents their place in the connection as you experience it, not access to their private thoughts; The dynamic looks at the pattern between you. Take what helps you think clearly and leave the rest.

Start with the situation you selected, then read each card as a prompt. A useful question is more valuable than a dramatic prediction. If a relationship feels unsafe or coercive, prioritize practical support and your safety over a tarot reading.

Questions to take into a conversation

  • What have I observed directly, and what am I assuming?
  • What do I need that I can express in a clear, respectful request?
  • What boundary or next step remains within my control?

For entertainment and self-reflection only. Tarot cannot confirm another person’s feelings, guarantee an outcome, or replace professional advice, consent, or honest communication.
