How It Works

Getting your love reading is simple, private, and always done with genuine care.

Booking a love reading shouldn't feel complicated, and with us it never does. You don't need to prepare a list of questions or know exactly what you're looking for before you begin — most people arrive with a feeling more than a plan, and that's enough to start a session.

Behind every session is a real psychic who has spent years focused specifically on matters of the heart: soulmate connections, breakups, compatibility, and the quiet doubts people rarely say out loud to anyone else. The three steps below are the entire process, start to finish, with nothing hidden in between.

1

Choose Your Reading

Browse our love reading types and select the one that resonates with your heart's current question. Not sure where to start? That's completely normal — most people know only that something feels unresolved, not which category it falls under. You can switch reading types mid-session if the conversation naturally moves somewhere else, and your psychic will follow your lead rather than stick to a script.

2

Connect with a Psychic

Meet your dedicated love psychic in a private chat or call, on your own schedule, day or night. Share what's on your heart in a safe, confidential space — no one else will ever see what you write or hear what you say. Take as much time as you need to explain the situation, because a good reading depends on the psychic understanding the real people and history behind your question, not just the surface of it.

3

Receive Your Guidance

Get clear, compassionate insights about your love life, delivered in plain language instead of vague riddles. Your psychic will walk you through what they're sensing, answer your follow-up questions, and suggest practical steps toward the outcome you're hoping for. Nothing here is a guarantee about the future — it's guidance meant to help you think clearly and choose your next move with more confidence. Many clients save their notes from a reading and return to them weeks later as things unfold.

A typical first reading runs between fifteen and thirty minutes, though there's no clock ticking over your shoulder. Some conversations wrap up in ten minutes because the answer was clear early on; others run longer because there's simply more to unpack. You set the pace, not us.

Your first session is completely free, and we mean that literally — no card on file, no auto-renewing subscription, no follow-up call from a salesperson. Create an account, choose a psychic who feels right for your situation, and start talking. If the connection isn't right, you're welcome to try a different psychic at no cost.

Readings are meant for insight, comfort, and a fresh perspective on your relationships, not as a substitute for professional medical, legal, or financial advice, and no psychic can guarantee a specific outcome. What we can promise is a caring, judgment-free space to talk through whatever is on your heart, available any hour of the day or night.

Our Methods

The Tools Our Readers Work With

Every reader draws on a different tradition. None of them replace your own judgment — they organize what you already sense but can't yet put into words.

A tool is a lens, not the source. Tarot cards don't contain your future any more than a compass contains your destination — they give a trained reader a structured way to look at your situation and reflect back patterns that are already there.

Different readers work differently. Some lean on symbolism and archetypes, some on timing and planetary cycles, some on numbers, and some read the emotional undercurrent of a situation directly. None of these approaches is more "real" than another — they're different vocabularies for the same kind of attention.

If you're not sure which fits your question, start with what draws you rather than what sounds most impressive. A confusing decision often suits tarot, a timing question often suits astrology, and a recurring pattern often suits numerology. If you mostly want to feel heard and understood before you decide anything, clairsentience is worth trying. You can also just tell your reader what's going on and let them suggest an approach — most readers are comfortable moving between methods within a single session.

Tarot: A Mirror for the Choice in Front of You

Tarot works well when you're standing at a fork in the road and need to see it clearly. Each card in a spread represents a piece of your situation — where you are, what's working against you, what you're not seeing, what's likely if you keep going the way you're going — and the reader's job is to read how those pieces sit next to each other and what story they add up to.

A tarot reading won't hand you a verdict, and a reader worth trusting will say so plainly. It gives you a structured look at the forces already in play, laid out where you can actually examine them, so the decision that follows is made with more information than you started with — and is still, in the end, yours to make.

Best for

  • Weighing a decision with no clear right answer
  • Understanding what's really going on beneath a conflict
  • Getting unstuck when you keep circling the same question
  • A quick gut-check before you commit to something big

Astrology & Synastry: Reading the Timing and the Fit

Astrology looks at two different things depending on your question. A birth chart maps your own patterns — how you tend to love, argue, and pull away when things get hard. Synastry compares two people's charts side by side to see where you naturally align, where you'll have to work harder for each other, and where friction is likely to keep resurfacing no matter how much you both want it to stop.

This tool is especially useful for timing questions — why now feels hard, or why a connection that started easily has suddenly gotten complicated — and for understanding compatibility beyond attraction alone, which fades faster than the deeper patterns astrology is built to track.

Best for

  • Understanding why a relationship's timing feels off
  • Comparing compatibility with a specific person
  • Making sense of a recurring relationship pattern
  • Deciding whether to push forward or step back right now
Ask an Astrology Reader

Numerology: Finding the Pattern in the Repeats

Numerology reads the numbers already present in your life — birth dates, recurring digits, the timing of when people enter and leave — as a record of pattern rather than coincidence. It's less about predicting an outcome and more about naming a cycle you're already living through, so it stops feeling random and starts feeling like something you can actually work with.

This works best when something keeps repeating and you want to understand why, not just cope with it again. A numerology reader can show you the shape of the cycle — where it tends to start, where it tends to break down — so you recognize it earlier next time instead of finding out you're in it once it's already too late.

Best for

  • Making sense of a relationship pattern that keeps repeating
  • Understanding compatibility through birth-date numbers
  • Deciding if this is the right season to make a move
  • Naming a cycle you've sensed but couldn't explain
Ask a Numerology Reader

Clairsentience: An Empathic Read on What You're Feeling

A clairsentient reader works by tuning into the emotional charge around your situation rather than a fixed system of cards or charts. If you already sense something but can't articulate it — a shift in someone's energy, a feeling you can't shake, a mood in the room you can't explain — this is the closest match to what you're already picking up on.

It's the most personal of the four approaches, less structured and more conversational, which makes it a good fit when what you need most, before you decide anything, is to feel truly heard by someone who isn't part of the situation.

Best for

  • Confirming a feeling you already sense but can't explain
  • Processing emotions after a hard conversation or breakup
  • Feeling heard when your situation is hard to put into words
  • Getting a read on the emotional tone of a connection

Our Values

How We Protect Your Trust

A reading only helps if you can be honest inside it. These are the standards we hold every session to.

Readers We Actually Vet

Every reader on Love Psychic goes through a screening conversation before they're allowed to take a session. We're checking for how they actually read — not a script, not a sales pitch, just whether they can sit with a real question, ask the right follow-up, and respond to what's actually there instead of what's easiest to say.

Readers who lean on fear, pressure, or vague generalities that could apply to anyone don't stay on the platform long — we check in on new readers after their first sessions and act on what clients tell us. We'd rather have fewer readers we trust completely than a long roster we don't.

Your Privacy Is Not a Setting

What you share in a session is yours, in the same way what you'd tell a friend in confidence is yours. We don't sell your conversation history, share it with advertisers, or publish excerpts anywhere, and your reader only sees what you choose to tell them in that session — nothing carries over unless you bring it up again.

You can request deletion of your personal data and reading records at any time, and we'll act on it. Records we do keep are held for up to twelve months and then permanently deleted — never longer than we need them. The full detail of what we store and for how long is set out in our privacy policy.

No Fear, No Upselling

We don't allow readers to invoke curses, blocked energy, or looming disasters to keep you booking more sessions, and we don't run countdown timers or "limited slots" messaging to rush a decision. If a reader's approach ever feels like pressure instead of insight, that's a problem we want to know about, not a sales tactic we quietly tolerate.

A reading is meant for insight and reflection, not medical, legal, or financial advice, and no reader can honestly guarantee a specific outcome.
