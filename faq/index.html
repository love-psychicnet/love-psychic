Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our love psychic readings.

Is the first reading really free?

Yes, absolutely. Your first love reading session is completely free with no hidden costs, no credit card required, and no obligation to continue. We believe everyone deserves access to compassionate love guidance, and this is our way of letting you experience our psychics’ gifts firsthand.

How do love psychic readings work?

Our love psychics use a combination of clairvoyance, empathic reading, and energy sensing to tune into the romantic energy surrounding your life. You share your question or situation, and your psychic connects with the energy to provide insights about your partner, relationship dynamics, timing of love events, and guidance for your romantic path forward.

Are the readings confidential?

100% confidential. We take your privacy extremely seriously. Nothing you share during a reading is recorded, stored, or shared with anyone — not even other psychics on our team. Your personal information is protected under our strict privacy policy, and all readings are conducted in a safe, judgment-free space.

Can a psychic tell me who my soulmate is?

Our soulmate specialists can tune into the energy of your destined partner and share insights about their personality, appearance, how you might meet, and the timing of your connection. While they can’t always provide a specific name, the details they reveal often help clients recognize their soulmate when they appear in their life.

What if I’m going through a breakup?

We have psychics who specialize specifically in breakup healing and emotional recovery. They can help you understand the spiritual lessons of your past relationship, release lingering emotional pain, and guide you through the healing process. Many clients find that a healing reading provides closure and peace that traditional methods can’t reach.

How do I choose the right psychic?

Each of our psychics has specific love specialties listed on their profile. Browse our psychic profiles and choose the one whose specialties align with your question. If you’re unsure, start with a general love reading and your psychic will guide you to the right focus area.

Are online psychic readings as accurate as in-person?

Yes. Psychic energy isn’t limited by physical distance. Our readers connect with your energy through intention and focus, which works equally well whether you’re in the same room or across the world. Many of our psychics actually prefer online readings because the digital space eliminates visual distractions and allows them to tune into pure energy.

How long does a love psychic reading take?

A standard free intro session lasts 5–10 minutes — enough time for your psychic to tune into your energy and answer your most pressing question. Full readings typically run 20–30 minutes for one focused topic, or 45–60 minutes for a deeper exploration of your love life. You always control the length and depth.

Can a psychic predict when my ex will come back?

Our readers can read the current energy between you and an ex-partner and identify the patterns that might lead to reconnection. While exact dates are rare, psychics often see windows of opportunity — and just as importantly, can tell you whether reconnection is in your highest good. Sometimes the more loving answer is “they won’t come back, but someone better will.”

What’s the difference between a soulmate and a twin flame?

Soulmates are deep, harmonious soul-level connections — you can have several across a lifetime, including friends and family. A twin flame is rarer: a single mirror soul that triggers intense personal growth, often through cycles of separation and reunion. Both deserve specialized readings — Isabella focuses on soulmates, Mira specializes in twin flame energy.

Do I need to prepare anything for my reading?

Just bring an open mind and one or two specific questions. You don’t need birth charts, photos, or names. Our readers tune into your energy directly — preparation that helps most is a moment of quiet beforehand and the willingness to hear honest answers, even if they’re different from what you expected.

Can a psychic tell me if my partner is cheating?

Our readers can sense energetic shifts that often accompany dishonesty — emotional distance, hidden anxiety, attachment to someone outside the relationship. They will share what they see, but the more powerful question we encourage clients to ask is “what does this connection feel like at a soul level, and is it aligned with the love I deserve?” That answer is almost always more useful than a yes-or-no.

What if I don’t feel a connection with my psychic?

Connection matters. If the energy doesn’t feel right within the first few minutes, you’re welcome to end the session and try a different reader at no charge. Every reader has a different communication style — finding the right match is part of the process, and we want you to find someone who feels like a guide, not a stranger.

Are love psychic readings accurate?

There is no scientific evidence that readings can predict events, and no honest reader will promise that they can. Many people find them useful as a structured way to reflect on feelings and choices. We present readings as guidance and entertainment, and we encourage you to weigh anything you hear against your own experience.

How should I choose a love psychic?

Read each reader’s profile and look for a style that appeals to you. Some people prefer a warm, conversational approach, while others like a more structured one. You can see each specialist on the love psychics page.

Can a reading help after a breakup?

It can offer a space to talk through what happened and what you want next. It will not undo a breakup or guarantee a reunion. If you are struggling with grief or low mood, speaking with a friend, doctor or counsellor is also important.

Should I ask about someone else’s feelings?

It is better to ask about your own part in a situation. Nobody can verify what another person is thinking, so questions such as “What do I need to understand about how we communicate?” lead to more helpful conversations.

What if I feel upset during a session?

Pause, breathe and tell the reader how you feel. You can end the session whenever you wish. Afterwards, spend time with someone you trust or take a walk. If you feel overwhelmed or unsafe, contact a doctor, a qualified counsellor or a local helpline.

Do readings replace therapy or counselling?

No. Readings are not a substitute for professional mental health care, medical advice or legal advice. They can sit alongside those supports as one more way to reflect.

How can I get the most from a session?

Come with one question, share only what you are comfortable with and take notes. Afterwards, choose one small step to try. Our relationship advice page has more ideas for preparing.
